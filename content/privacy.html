2. Information TagTeam handles

  • Parent accounts and family connections: names, email addresses, account identifiers, email-bound connection invitations, email verification and authentication records, accepted policy versions, trial dates and the relationship between the two connected accounts.
  • Children and care: names, dates of birth, recurring morning and evening routines, events, dates, descriptions, uploaded event files and proposed or confirmed changes.
  • Expenses: receipt images, amounts, expense categories and reasons, the uploading parent, dates, split ratios, payment due dates, payment notes and confirmation records. TagTeam records payment status; it does not move money between parents.
  • Medical records and documents: child selections, medical categories, descriptions, dates, attachments, birth certificates, medical aid documents and other files you choose to upload.
  • Activity and technical records: who added, amended, confirmed or requested deletion of a record, timestamps, device-side settings, queued changes and technical information necessary for security and service operation.
  • Subscriptions: trial and paid-access dates, a hash of your normalised email to prevent repeat trials, checkout references, Payfast payment and subscription identifiers, amounts, payment status and cancellation records. An email hash is still treated as personal information. Payment details are entered at Payfast; TagTeam does not receive your full card number or banking password.
  • Optional weather location: your last chosen approximate location, rounded before being saved on your phone and used to request a forecast. It is not part of the shared family record.
  • Website and in-app enquiries: your name or account email, enquiry topic, message and consent to use those details to respond. The in-app form also uses your account identifier for access and abuse controls. Hosting and email providers may process IP addresses, browser information and delivery logs.

We do not need your banking password, full payment-card details, private messages unrelated to the app or a child’s identity number to answer an ordinary support enquiry. Please keep sensitive family information out of website and in-app support enquiries.

3. Children’s information and medical records

TagTeam accounts are intended for adults aged 18 or older. Children do not create accounts in the current app. A parent or guardian must have lawful authority to provide a child’s information and obtain any consent required from a competent person under POPIA. Health information also needs an applicable lawful basis for its processing.

Upload only information needed for shared care. Connecting another adult to a family gives that adult access to its shared records. Confirm their identity and authority before connecting. Tell us promptly if authority changes, sharing becomes inappropriate or a child’s information has been included without proper authority.

An in-app confirmation records a parent’s response to a proposal. It does not independently establish legal guardianship, verify consent for every use of the information or replace a medical professional’s assessment.

4. Why information is used

Information is used to provide accounts, connect the intended parents, synchronise family records, calculate expense shares, support confirmations, keep an activity history, show reminders, manage trials and subscriptions, prevent repeat-trial or payment abuse, answer enquiries and protect service integrity. Processing relies on the basis relevant to the activity, including providing the requested service, consent where required, legitimate interests where lawful, and legal obligations.

Receipt text recognition runs on the phone in the current Android app. You review the detected total before saving it. The receipt and saved details are then shared through your family’s connected service. TagTeam does not make automated legal, healthcare or parenting decisions about your family.

Weather is optional. TagTeam first asks for your consent, then asks Android for permission to obtain your approximate location while using the app. The last selected location is stored on your phone and sent through the TagTeam service to Open-Meteo for forecasts. It is not stored in family database tables or shared with your co-parent. TagTeam does not request precise or background location tracking. You can decline and continue using the other features, update the saved location when you travel, or remove it in Family and revoke Android permission.

6. Storage, device access and security

TagTeam uses encrypted network connections, database access rules that restrict shared records to their family, and private Azure file storage. File upload and retrieval require the service to check the signed-in account, family, active access and associated record. Files do not use public download links. Sessions, cached records, receipts, documents and attachments are encrypted in account-specific app storage using Android Keystore-backed protection. The app blocks screenshots; camera and PDF processing may use short-lived private cache files. We do not describe TagTeam as end-to-end encrypted. Authorised service operators and processors can handle information for the purposes described above.

The Documents section uses your phone’s screen lock to unlock access. TagTeam does not receive your fingerprint or face template. Keep your phone locked, secure your account and install updates. Avoid sharing an unlocked phone or your sign-in credentials.

Documents, PDF reports and backups you explicitly download or export are ordinary unencrypted files outside the app’s protected storage. The other parent may also retain lawful copies. Removing a record or closing an account cannot automatically erase files already exported to another device, email account or backup.

No system is completely secure. If a security compromise affects personal information, we will assess it and provide notices to the Information Regulator and affected people as required by applicable law.
