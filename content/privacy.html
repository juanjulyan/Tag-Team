2. Information TagTeam handles

  • Parent accounts and family connections: names, email addresses, account identifiers, connection invitations, authentication records and the relationship between the two connected accounts.
  • Children and care: names, dates of birth, recurring morning and evening routines, events, dates, descriptions, uploaded event files and proposed or confirmed changes.
  • Expenses: receipt images, amounts, expense categories and reasons, the uploading parent, dates, split ratios, payment due dates, payment notes and confirmation records. TagTeam records payment status; it does not move money between parents.
  • Medical records and documents: child selections, medical categories, descriptions, dates, attachments, birth certificates, medical aid documents and other files you choose to upload.
  • Activity and technical records: who added, amended, confirmed or requested deletion of a record, timestamps, device-side settings, queued changes and technical information necessary for security and service operation.
  • Website enquiries: your name, email address, enquiry topic, message and your agreement to receive a response. Hosting and email providers may process IP addresses, browser information and delivery logs.

We do not need your banking password, full payment-card details, private messages unrelated to the app or a child’s identity number to answer an ordinary support enquiry. Please keep sensitive family information out of the public website’s contact form.

3. Children’s information and medical records

TagTeam accounts are intended for adults aged 18 or older. Children do not create accounts in the current app. A parent or guardian must have lawful authority to provide a child’s information and obtain any consent required from a competent person under POPIA. Health information also needs an applicable lawful basis for its processing.

Upload only information needed for shared care. Connecting another adult to a family gives that adult access to its shared records. Confirm their identity and authority before connecting. Tell us promptly if authority changes, sharing becomes inappropriate or a child’s information has been included without proper authority.

An in-app confirmation records a parent’s response to a proposal. It does not independently establish legal guardianship, verify consent for every use of the information or replace a medical professional’s assessment.

4. Why information is used

Information is used to provide accounts, connect the intended parents, synchronise family records, calculate expense shares, support confirmations, keep an activity history, show reminders, answer enquiries and protect service integrity. Processing relies on the basis relevant to the activity, including providing the requested service, consent where required, legitimate interests where lawful, and legal obligations.

Receipt text recognition runs on the phone in the current Android app. You review the detected total before saving it. The receipt and saved details are then shared through your family’s connected service. TagTeam does not make automated legal, healthcare or parenting decisions about your family.

The current weather feature requests forecasts for a fixed Durbanville location. It does not need continuous tracking of your phone’s precise location.

6. Storage, device access and security

TagTeam uses encrypted network connections to its shared service and access rules intended to separate families. Supported documents and medical attachments are encrypted inside the Android app’s local storage. Other locally cached records and receipts rely on app-private storage and your device’s protections. We do not describe TagTeam as end-to-end encrypted.

The Documents section uses your phone’s screen lock to unlock access. TagTeam does not receive your fingerprint or face template. Keep your phone locked, secure your account and install updates. Avoid sharing an unlocked phone or your sign-in credentials.

Documents you download or export are outside the app’s protected storage. The other parent may also retain lawful copies. Removing a record or closing an account cannot automatically erase files already exported to another device, email account or backup.

No system is completely secure. If a security compromise affects personal information, we will assess it and provide notices to the Information Regulator and affected people as required by applicable law.
